I understand how matter behaves at the nanoscale. What comes next, I'm still writing.

My background is in physics — seven years doing experimental research across biomaterials, organic semiconductors, and stem cell biology at Politecnico di Milano, IIT Milano and UC Irvine. The work was real and the problems were hard. The frontier moved, and I pushed past it.

In the meantime I've been building: Python apps integrating LLMs into actual workflows, all shipped solo, all on GitHub. Picked up certifications in project management and process improvement along the way. None of it is accidental.

The trajectory: deep tech, but closer to where the decisions get made. The science is the foundation. What gets built on top is the interesting part.
